|------|-----|

- **Database:** PostgreSQL
- **Cache:** Redis (up to 24 hours)
- **Logs:** 30 days
- **Deletion:** After 30 days of inactivity → automatic cleanup

**We do NOT sell or transfer data to third parties** (except Telegram API for bot operation).

## 🚫 Source blocking at author's request
If you are a content author (Telegram channel or YouTube channel) and do not want MyAggryBot to parse your content:

1. **Contact the developer** — write to support @
2. **Specify your channel** — username or link
3. **We will block the source** — all subscriptions and assignments will be removed

After blocking:
- ⛔ The channel is permanently excluded from parsing
- 🗑️ All subscriptions and assignments are deleted
- 🔒 If a user tries to add the channel again — they will receive a blocking notification

**We respect content authors' rights and respond promptly to requests.**

- Add channels with 18+, violent, or spam content
- Try to bypass limits
- Exploit bot vulnerabilities
**In case of violation:** blocking without warning
